# Campus LAN (Phase 3 domain stub)

Vendor-neutral campus switching / LAN domain guidance.

## When this domain applies
- Access/distribution/core switching, VLANs, wired ports, or campus fabric requirements.

## Typical dependencies
- Identity and Cybersecurity for access control
- Monitoring / Observability for operations

## Selection notes
Keep design vendor-neutral. Port counts and PoE needs become capacity/open questions later.
