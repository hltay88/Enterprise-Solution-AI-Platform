# Audio Visual (AV) Clarification Pack

Use for meeting rooms, boardrooms, auditoriums, training rooms, or AV system refresh (non-LED-wall primary).

## Rooms & use cases
- Room list with seating capacity and primary use (huddle, board, hybrid meeting, lecture)?
- Bring-your-own-device vs room-native UC (Teams/Zoom/Meet) expectations?
- Recording / streaming / lecture-capture needs?

## Audio
- Microphone coverage model (ceiling, table, wireless) and acoustics concerns?
- DSP, AEC, and voice-lift / speech-reinforcement requirements?
- Assistive listening or multilingual interpretation needs?

## Video & switching
- Display sizes/locations and confidence monitors?
- Source types (laptop, room PC, document camera, IPTV)?
- Matrix / presentation switching and BYOD wireless presentation?

## Control & cabling
- Control UI expectations (touch panel, table buttons, automation)?
- Cable pathways, rack locations, and equipment-room readiness?
- Integration with lighting, shades, or room booking panels?

## Support
- Training for facilities/IT and SLA for break-fix?
- Manufacturer / integrator warranty split?
