# Wireless / WLAN Presales Clarification Pack

Use this checklist when the opportunity involves Wi‑Fi, WLAN, wireless access, access points, heatmap, RF survey, or campus wireless.

## Site & drawings
- Is an accurate floor plan available (PDF/CAD) with scale and north arrow?
- Which buildings, floors, and outdoor areas are in scope?
- Are restricted / no-coverage zones defined (exam halls, Faraday rooms, etc.)?

## Coverage objectives
- What are the targeted wireless coverage areas (rooms, corridors, auditoriums, hostels, outdoors)?
- What minimum signal level is required (e.g. -65 dBm data, -67 dBm voice)?
- Is outdoor / courtyard / parking coverage required?

## Capacity & clients
- Expected concurrent clients per area / peak density (students, staff, guests)?
- Primary device types (laptops, phones, IoT, scanners)?
- Peak-usage scenarios (exam periods, events, registration day)?

## RF / environment
- Ceiling height, wall/floor materials, and known RF challenges?
- Existing WLAN vendors, channels, and interference sources?
- Any medical / lab / industrial RF constraints?

## Design deliverables
- Is a predictive heatmap / RF design required in the proposal?
- Should estimated AP quantity and AP types be provided from floor-plan sizing?
- Is an on-site validation / AP-on-a-stick survey required before BOM freeze?

## Infrastructure readiness
- Is structured cabling / PoE switch capacity available at proposed AP locations?
- Preferred mounting (ceiling, wall, enclosure) and aesthetic constraints?
- Power / UPS expectations for wireless infrastructure?

## Network & security services
- Required SSIDs, VLANs, guest/captive portal, and 802.1X/NAC model?
- Roaming / voice / video quality expectations?
- Integration with existing firewall, DHCP, DNS, and identity systems?

## Commercial / delivery
- Warranty, TAC, and managed service expectations?
- Phased rollout by building/floor?
- Who approves the RF design and AP count (customer IT / consultant / OEM)?
