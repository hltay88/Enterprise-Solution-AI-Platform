# Storage Clarification Pack

Use for SAN, NAS, object storage, all-flash arrays, or storage refresh/consolidation.

## Workloads
- Which applications/workloads will land on the new storage (DB, VMs, file, backup, AI/media)?
- Required capacity now and growth over 3–5 years?
- Performance targets (IOPS, latency, throughput) per workload class?

## Data services
- Snapshot, replication, tiering, and ransomware-protection expectations?
- Protocols required (FC, iSCSI, NVMe-oF, NFS, SMB, S3)?
- Multi-site / DR RPO-RTO targets?

## Environment
- Existing storage to migrate or coexist with — and migration tooling constraints?
- Fabric/switch readiness (FC/Ethernet) and multipathing standards?
- Rack space, power, and cooling headroom for the array?

## Operations
- Who administers storage day-2?
- Monitoring / alerting / chargeback requirements?
- Support tier and spare-parts expectations?
