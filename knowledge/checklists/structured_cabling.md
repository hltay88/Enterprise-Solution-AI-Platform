# Structured Cabling Clarification Pack

Use for copper/fibre cabling, containment, patching, or campus backbone cabling projects.

## Scope
- Buildings/floors/IDF-MDF pairs in scope?
- New install vs remediation of existing cabling?
- Target standards (Cat6/Cat6A/OM3/OM4/OS2) and channel lengths?

## Pathways
- Existing containment capacity and fire-stopping constraints?
- Underground / outdoor fibre routes and permits?
- Labelling and documentation standards required?

## Delivery
- Cutover constraints and occupied-building work rules?
- Testing certification deliverables (Fluke reports, as-builts)?
- Warranty (system warranty) expectations?
