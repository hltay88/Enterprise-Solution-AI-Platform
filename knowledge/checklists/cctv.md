# CCTV / Video Surveillance Clarification Pack

Use for IP CCTV, VMS, ANPR, or surveillance refresh projects.

## Coverage
- Camera schedule by location (indoor/outdoor) and purpose (overview, face, ANPR)?
- Required resolution, FPS, and low-light/IR expectations?
- Retention days and archive policy?

## Architecture
- Edge recording vs centralized NVR/VMS and HA needs?
- Bandwidth impact on campus LAN/WAN?
- Cyber hardening for cameras/VMS (VLAN, certificates, hardening)?

## Operations
- Who monitors live video (security ops hours)?
- Integration with access control / alarm / PSIM?
- Privacy / signage / PDPA considerations?
