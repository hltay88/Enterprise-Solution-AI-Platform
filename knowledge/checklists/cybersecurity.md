# Cybersecurity Clarification Pack

Use for endpoint, identity, SOC/SIEM, zero trust program, XDR/MDR, PAM, or broader cyber controls (beyond pure network firewalling).

## Business & risk
- What critical assets / crown jewels must be protected first?
- Known incidents, audit findings, or board-driven deadlines?
- Risk acceptance appetite vs mandatory controls?

## Endpoint & workload
- Endpoint estate size/OS mix (Windows/macOS/Linux/OT) and current EDR/AV?
- Server / cloud workload protection expectations?
- Patch / vulnerability management ownership today?

## Identity & zero trust
- Current IdP and MFA coverage gaps?
- Privileged access (PAM/JIT) requirements?
- Conditional access / device posture expectations?

## Detect & respond
- Is a SOC in-house, MSSP, or to-be-built?
- SIEM/XDR use cases and use-case prioritization?
- Incident response runbooks, tabletop, and SLA targets?

## Compliance
- Frameworks to evidence (ISO 27001, NIST, PCI-DSS, PDPA, etc.)?
- Logging retention and evidence packaging needs?
