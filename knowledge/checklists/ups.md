# UPS / Power Protection Clarification Pack

Use for UPS, PDU, generator interface, or power protection for IT/AV/DC loads.

## Load
- Critical load inventory (kW/kVA) and growth allowance?
- Runtime requirement at full/typical load?
- Single-phase vs three-phase and input voltage?

## Architecture
- Topology (online double-conversion, modular, redundant N+1/2N)?
- Battery chemistry preference and replacement cycle?
- Integration with generator / ATS and bypass needs?

## Facilities & ops
- Floor loading, clearances, and ventilation/HVAC impact?
- Monitoring (SNMP/Modbus) and alerting destinations?
- Maintenance contract and battery testing cadence?
