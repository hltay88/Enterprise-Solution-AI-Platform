# Access Control Clarification Pack

Use for door access, turnstiles, visitor management, or physical identity projects.

## Sites & doors
- Door/turnstile count by site and security level?
- Credential types (card, mobile, biometric) and existing badge estate?
- Offline door behaviour and anti-passback needs?

## Integration
- HR/identity source of truth for joiners-movers-leavers?
- Visitor management / contractor workflows?
- Integration with CCTV, alarms, or building management?

## Compliance & ops
- Audit trail / reporting requirements?
- Who administers access day-2?
- Fail-secure vs fail-safe life-safety constraints?
