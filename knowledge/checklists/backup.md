# Backup & Recovery Clarification Pack

Use for backup software/appliances, immutable backup, or recovery posture improvement.

## Scope
- What must be backed up (VMs, databases, SaaS, endpoints, NAS, cloud)?
- Current backup tool pain points and retention gaps?
- RPO/RTO targets by workload tier?

## Architecture
- On-prem, cloud, or hybrid backup repository strategy?
- Immutability / air-gap / ransomware recovery requirements?
- Bandwidth and backup-window constraints?

## Restore & compliance
- Required restore test frequency and evidence reporting?
- Legal hold / long-term archive needs?
- Who owns backup admin and restore authorization?
