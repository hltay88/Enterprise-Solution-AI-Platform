# Collaboration / Unified Communications Clarification Pack

Use for Microsoft 365 collaboration, meeting-room UC, telephony, or UC migration.

## Users & workloads
- User count and personas (knowledge workers, frontline, contact centre)?
- Required workloads (Exchange, Teams, SharePoint, Viva, calling)?
- External collaboration / guest access needs?

## Meetings & rooms
- Number of rooms needing Teams/Zoom rooms hardware?
- Hybrid meeting quality issues to solve (audio/video)?
- Phone system / PSTN calling model (Calling Plans, Direct Routing, PBX retain)?

## Identity & compliance
- Tenant readiness, licensing SKUs available, and identity sync model?
- Retention, eDiscovery, DLP, and sensitivity-label requirements?
- Change-management / adoption support expectations?
