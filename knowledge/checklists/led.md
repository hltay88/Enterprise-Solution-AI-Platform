# LED Video Wall Clarification Pack

Use for LED walls, fine-pitch displays, control-room video walls, or event LED canvases.

## Viewing & content
- Primary use case (lobby brand, NOC/SOC, auditorium, retail, events)?
- Typical viewing distance and audience positions?
- Content types (HDMI sources, CMS, PC walls, broadcast, data viz)?

## Physical design
- Exact wall dimensions / aspect ratio and mounting surface condition?
- Indoor vs outdoor, ambient light, and brightness targets?
- Structural load approval and service access (front vs rear service)?

## Pixel & processing
- Required pixel pitch / resolution and seamlessness expectations?
- Processor / controller redundancy and failover needs?
- HDR, refresh rate, or genlock requirements?

## Integration & ops
- Source switching / matrix / control system integration?
- CMS / scheduling / remote management expectations?
- Power feeds, heat rejection, and spare-module strategy?
- Warranty, calibration, and preventive maintenance model?
