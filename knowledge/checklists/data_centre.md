# Data Centre Clarification Pack

Use when the opportunity involves a data centre / data center build, refresh, colo fit-out, or DC migration.

## Scope & site
- Is this a new build, refresh, expansion, or migration (and from where)?
- Which rooms/halls/racks are in scope (white space, staging, NOC)?
- Target go-live date and any hard freeze windows?

## Space & layout
- Available white-space dimensions, rack count, and preferred row layout?
- Raised floor vs slab, ceiling height, and cable pathway constraints?
- Weight limits and loading-dock / lift access for equipment?

## Power & cooling
- Available UPS/PDU capacity, redundancy model (N, N+1, 2N), and target kW per rack?
- Cooling type (CRAC/CRAH, in-row, rear-door) and current thermal headroom?
- Generator / utility feed ownership and testing schedule?

## Connectivity
- Carrier/meet-me room access and required WAN/LAN handoff points?
- Structured cabling standard (Cat6A/OM4/etc.) and patching ownership?
- Cross-connect / dark fibre requirements between halls or sites?

## Operations
- Who operates the DC day-2 (customer, colo, MSP)?
- Monitoring, DCIM, and environmental sensor expectations?
- Compliance / tier / audit requirements (Uptime, ISO, PCI zone, etc.)?
