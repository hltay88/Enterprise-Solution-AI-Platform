# Digital Signage Clarification Pack

Use for digital signage networks, wayfinding, menu boards, or CMS-driven displays (non-fine-pitch LED wall).

## Content & screens
- Screen count/locations and orientation (landscape/portrait)?
- Content ownership and update frequency?
- Required CMS features (scheduling, zones, live data, emergency override)?

## Network & players
- Media player model (SoC display apps vs external players)?
- Network VLAN / internet dependency and offline playback needs?
- Remote monitoring and proof-of-play reporting?

## Physical
- Mounting, power, and ambient-light constraints?
- Warranty and onsite SLAs for public areas?
