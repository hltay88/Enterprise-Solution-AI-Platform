# Wireless — mandatory questions (stub)

1. Which buildings/floors need coverage, and outdoor areas?
2. Expected concurrent clients per floor / zone?
3. Required SSIDs and authentication method (802.1X, PSK, guest portal)?
4. Are voice / video / IoT devices in scope?
5. Is a predictive or on-site RF survey required before install?
6. What roaming / latency acceptance criteria apply?
