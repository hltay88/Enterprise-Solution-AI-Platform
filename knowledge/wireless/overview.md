# Wireless / WLAN Knowledge Pack (stub)

Vendor-neutral Wi‑Fi discovery guidance for enterprise campus and hospitality use cases.

## Typical business problems
- Coverage holes and sticky clients
- Insufficient capacity for concurrent users
- Guest vs corporate SSID separation unclear
- Roaming / voice / video quality issues

## Discovery focus
- Floors, wall materials, and outdoor areas
- Concurrent client counts and device mix
- Required SSIDs, authentication (802.1X / captive portal)
- Integration with existing wired switching and NAC
- Survey / heatmap expectations and acceptance criteria
