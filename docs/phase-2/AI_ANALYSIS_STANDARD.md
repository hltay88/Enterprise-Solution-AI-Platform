# AI_ANALYSIS_STANDARD.md

Project Atlas AI Analysis Standard

---

## Golden Rule

Understand before recommending.

---

## AI Workflow

```text
Read Documents
  ↓
Extract Text
  ↓
Normalize Content
  ↓
Identify Business Context
  ↓
Extract Requirements
  ↓
Categorize Requirements
  ↓
Extract Constraints
  ↓
Extract Risks
  ↓
Extract Assumptions
  ↓
Generate Clarification Questions
  ↓
Calculate Completeness Score
  ↓
Calculate Confidence Score
  ↓
Generate Requirement Knowledge Model
  ↓
AI Validation
  ↓
Human Review
  ↓
Approval
  ↓
Publish Requirement Knowledge Model
  ↓
STOP
```

---

## AI Principles

- Never hallucinate.
- Never invent customer requirements.
- Never assume technical specifications.
- Always preserve original customer intent.
- Always explain confidence.
- Always identify uncertainty.
- Always ask clarification questions when required.
- Never recommend vendors during Phase 2.
- Never recommend products during Phase 2.
- Never generate architecture during Phase 2.
