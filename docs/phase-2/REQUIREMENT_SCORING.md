# REQUIREMENT_SCORING.md

**Module:** Requirement Quality Assessment

---

## Purpose

Measure how complete and trustworthy a Requirement Knowledge Model is.

---

## Scores

- Completeness Score
- Confidence Score
- Consistency Score
- Evidence Coverage
- Risk Score
- Business Understanding
- Technical Understanding
- Overall Quality

---

| Non Functional Requirements | 20% |
| Constraints | 10% |

| Risks | 10% |

- Evidence Coverage

---

## Quality Levels

| Score | Level |
|-------|-------|
| 95–100 | Enterprise Ready |
| 90–94 | Excellent |
| 80–89 | Good |
| 70–79 | Requires Review |
| Below 70 | Incomplete |

---

## Publishing Rules

Only Requirement Knowledge Models with:

- Completeness ≥ 85
- Confidence ≥ 85
- Human Approval

may be published.
