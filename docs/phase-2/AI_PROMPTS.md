# AI_PROMPTS.md

**Prompt Library**  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Prompt 1 — Requirement Extraction

**Goal:** Extract business and technical requirements without recommending products.  
**Output:** Requirement Knowledge Model.

---

## Prompt 2 — Requirement Classification

**Goal:** Assign each requirement to one primary category and priority.

---

## Prompt 3 — Gap Analysis

**Goal:** Identify missing information required before architecture design.  
**Output:** Clarification questions with priority.

---

## Prompt 4 — Evidence Mapping

**Goal:** Link every requirement to supporting evidence.  
**Output:** Evidence references including document, page, and excerpt.

---

## Prompt 5 — Consistency Review

**Goal:** Detect conflicting requirements across multiple documents.  
**Output:** Conflict report with affected requirements.

---

## Prompt 6 — Executive Summary

**Goal:** Summarize customer objectives and key challenges from the approved RKM.  
**Restriction:** Do not recommend vendors or products.

---

## Prompt Governance

All prompts shall:

- Preserve customer intent.
- Explain uncertainty.
- Avoid hallucinations.
- Produce structured output.
- Be version controlled.
