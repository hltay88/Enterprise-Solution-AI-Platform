# KNOWLEDGE_PACK_STANDARD.md
Module: Enterprise Knowledge Library — Version 1.0

---

## Purpose
Knowledge Packs provide reusable engineering knowledge that supports AI reasoning.
Knowledge Packs do not replace the Requirement Knowledge Model — they enrich AI decisions.
