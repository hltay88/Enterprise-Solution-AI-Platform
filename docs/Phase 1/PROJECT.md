# 1. Executive Summary  
  
Project Atlas is an AI-powered Enterprise Solution Platform designed to assist Presales Engineers, Solution Architects, Enterprise Architects, IT Consultants, and System Integrators throughout the complete enterprise solution lifecycle.  
  
Instead of generating documents directly from customer requirements, Project Atlas follows a structured consulting methodology.  
  
The AI first understands the customer's business objectives, identifies missing information, asks clarification questions, recommends solution domains, and only then generates technical deliverables.  
  
The platform is designed to be AI-provider independent and compatible with multiple Large Language Models (LLMs), including OpenAI, Anthropic, Azure OpenAI, and future providers.  
  
---  
  
# 2. Product Vision  
  
The long-term objective of Project Atlas is to become the digital co-pilot for enterprise solution teams.  
  
Rather than replacing Solution Architects, the platform augments their expertise by reducing repetitive work while maintaining professional engineering standards.  
  
The platform should eventually support:  
  
- Requirement Intelligence  
- Architecture Design  
- Proposal Generation  
- Presentation Generation  
- Statement of Work (SOW)  
- Bill of Materials (BOM)  
- Risk Assessment  
- Compliance Validation  
- Multi-vendor Recommendation  
- Knowledge Management  
- AI-assisted Cost Estimation

# 5. Business Problems  
  
Today's enterprise solution development process is highly manual.  
  
Typical activities include:  
  
- Reading customer requirements  
- Conducting clarification sessions  
- Preparing solution designs  
- Comparing vendors  
- Creating proposals  
- Building PowerPoint presentations  
- Writing Statements of Work  
- Producing implementation plans  
  
These activities are time-consuming, difficult to standardize, and heavily dependent on individual experience.  
  
Project Atlas aims to streamline this workflow while keeping expert review at the center of the process.  
  
---  
  
# 6. Guiding Principles  
  
1. Solve the business problem before recommending technology.  
2. Recommend solution domains before recommending vendors.  
3. Every recommendation must include supporting reasoning.  
4. AI supports human decision-making; it does not replace it.  
5. Every AI-generated document must be editable by humans.  
6. Maintain vendor neutrality wherever possible.  
7. Design for extensibility and modularity.
